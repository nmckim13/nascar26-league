About the
N26 League.

Real NASCAR points. Real teams. Real playoffs. A real trophy with your name on it — for the price of a video game and a couch. This is everything you need to know.

How It Works
Draft Night to Champion's Circle.
1
Live Draft Night
Before the season starts, team managers hold a live snake draft. They pick their 3-driver rosters from the full pool. Your name gets called. You represent a real NASCAR team. The season starts.
2
10-Week Regular Season
One race per week. Real NASCAR Cup Series points — 40 for the win, descending to 1st for 40th. Stage breaks pay bonus points mid-race. Every position, every lap counts. Nobody is ever mathematically out of it.
3
The Championship Chase
Top 10 drivers after Race 7 advance to the Chase. The regular season points leader carries a bonus in. The final 3 races — Races 8, 9, and 10 — decide the champion. No excuses, no do-overs.
4
Champions Crowned
Driver Championship trophy. Owner Championship trophy. And a traveling trophy that gets a new name engraved after every season. Ten years from now, that thing is a piece of history.
Points System
Real NASCAR Rules.
44
Win Bonus
40 finish points + 4 win bonus. Most points you can earn in a single race.
40–1
Finish Points
Every position pays. 40 for 1st down to 1 for 40th. 15th still matters.
10–1
Stage Points
Top 10 at each stage break earn bonus points. Two stage breaks per race.
2
Championships
Driver Championship and Owner Championship run simultaneously all season.

Key Rules
The Important Stuff.
📅
75% Attendance
Drivers must race in at least 6 of 7 regular season races to qualify for the Chase. Miss too many and you're watching from the sideline.
🔄
Substitutes Welcome
Can't make a race? Your team manager finds a sub. The sub's points count 100% toward the team Owner Championship. Life happens — the league rolls on.
🐍
Snake Draft
Live snake draft before every season. Order reverses each round so no team always picks first. Show up to draft night — it's an event.
🏆
Chase Seeding
The regular season points leader carries a bonus into the Chase. Dominating the regular season has real meaning.
👥
Owner Championship
All 3 drivers' points combined each week. A team can win the Owner Championship even if no individual driver wins the Driver Championship.
