# BARL truck artwork

Craftsman Truck Series body-style illustrations for the driver-card artwork. The original stock-car assets remain under `assets/cars/` for reference and possible restoration.

Each image uses a consistent front-left three-quarter camera, landscape canvas, and transparent background. Names and profile links remain live HTML.

Arthur's #3 uses a black-dominant Silverado with vivid orange tiger stripes and white number decals, without a primary sponsor. Its hood, lower sides and roof have broader black areas, with BARL windshield branding. Hendrick membership is unchanged. The #9 art remains available for historical results; the separate renumbering migration preserves all prior results and seat identities.

Matt's #5 is a custom yellow Zep Silverado with blue-and-yellow hot-rod flames, thin white pinstripes, and blue/white number 5 decals. The sponsor and card accent follow this livery; his number, team and race history are unchanged.

The truck library includes numbers 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 14, 17, 19, 20, 23, 24, 42, 54, 60, 88, 93 and 97. Chevrolet schemes use Silverado bodies, Ford schemes use F-150 bodies, Toyota schemes use Tundra bodies, and #14 uses a Ram 1500 body. The existing BARL colors and sponsors are adapted onto racing pickups; these are AI illustrations, not official game exports or claims of real Truck Series entries. Brantley's #93 uses Caleb Costner's white/camo Piedmont Outfitters / Realtree scheme with burnt-orange number artwork; the old #97 artwork remains available for historical views. Ethan's #14 uses Mini Tyrrell's black/yellow RAM scheme with white door numbers and yellow roof numbers, retaining his RFK team membership. The #60 artwork and number styles remain for historical views.

The #88 matches Nolan's supplied white/orange/green 7-Eleven NASCAR 26 truck, with red Hendrick-style 88s on the door and roof matching his number badge. The badge uses the Dale Earnhardt Jr. Hendrick numeral silhouette recolored red with white outlines and transparent counters. The #42 uses the Pastrana-style BRUNT livery and the reference-derived number badge. #23 receives a Hardee's truck rather than a vehicle-art placeholder. Generation prompts and source paths are recorded in `prompts.json`. Web exports use transparent WebP at quality 86, 1536×1024.

`data/car-art.js` supplies explicit vehicle image paths and truck model names. `scripts/profile-car-card.js` keeps driver names, badges and career links separate from the generated art. Profile cards use a taller 1.8:1 stage with centered framing to show the full truck silhouette. The homepage winner illustration uses the new #88 truck.
