The Details

Built Like the
Real Thing

Points System

  • 40 points for the win, scaling down to 1st for 40th place
  • Win bonus — winner gets 44 total (40 + 4 bonus)
  • Stage points — top 10 at each stage break earn 10-to-1 bonus points
  • Two championships — Driver and Owner (Team) run simultaneously
  • Owner points — sum of all 3 team drivers each race

Teams & Draft

  • 6 teams of 3 drivers each — Season 1 target
  • Real NASCAR team identities — 23XI, Hendrick, Penske, Front Row, and more
  • Live snake draft before every season — order reverses each round
  • Team Managers recruit, mentor, and find subs when life happens
  • Subs are welcome — a car on track is a car on track

The Chase (Playoffs)

  • Top 10 drivers after Race 7 advance to the Championship Chase
  • Regular season leader carries a bonus into the Chase
  • Final 3 races determine the champion
  • 75% attendance required to qualify — 6 of 7 regular season races
  • Can't make a race? Your team finds you a sub. No excuses needed.

The Season Cycle

  • ~10 weeks per season — short enough to stay intense
  • Multiple seasons per year — fresh draft, new rivalries
  • Results posted within 24 hours of every race
  • Live standings updated all season on the league website
  • Scales to 40 drivers — built for growth from day one
