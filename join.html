Season 2 / O’Reilly Auto Parts Series

Ten rounds.
Every kind of fight.

Seven races to earn your place. Three races to chase the title. From Daytona to Chicagoland, here’s the ten-round slate for BARL’s move to the NASCAR O’Reilly Auto Parts Series.

The top 12 eligible full-time drivers advance on regular-season points. One NASCAR-seeded reset after Round 7. Three Chase races. No eliminations. No dropped scores. See the Chase rules and reset points →

Proposed slate · Dates to be announced. Tracks and running order are not final. This preview does not publish an official database season.

    Round 01 / Superspeedway

    Daytona

    One pack. A whole new pecking order. Start your story on the biggest stage.

  2. 02

    Round 02 / Short track

    Iowa

    Less room. More pressure. Make the outside line your own.

  3. 03

    Round 03 / Concrete oval

    Dover

    Tame the Monster Mile. Build your rhythm before the Chase takes shape.

    Round 04 / Road course

    Watkins Glen

    Both directions. No shortcuts. Who can put a complete lap together?

    Round 05 / One-mile oval

    Rockingham

    Back to the Rock. Save your tires and make the long run yours.

  6. 06

    Round 06 / Short track

    Thunder Valley. Tight quarters. Nowhere to hide from the next battle.

  7. 07Regular-season finale

    Round 07 / Concrete oval

  8. 08Chase opener

    Round 08 / Chase 1 of 3

    Phoenix

    The points reset. The pressure rises. Launch your title challenge in the desert.

  9. 09Chase continues

    Round 09 / Chase 2 of 3

    One last long run. One title on the line. Finish the Chase in the Windy City.
