Official Rulebook — Season 1

Get your
crew in.

N26 is a fantasy NASCAR league built on NASCAR 26. Real teams. Real points. Real competition — with your friends. Grab a car, pick your number, and race a full season from Daytona to Homestead.

How It Works
Get Your Crew Together
It Starts With Your Group

Grab 2–3 of your friends, pick a NASCAR team, and race together under that banner all season. You each claim one car from the same team and compete — both individually for the Driver Championship and collectively for the Owner Championship.

Want the same team as your boys? You can have it. Hendrick, JGR, Penske, 23XI, RFK, Spire, Trackhouse — every major organization is in the game. Max 3 drivers per team, so move fast.

Two Ways to Win

Driver Championship — Every player competes individually. Your car's real-world finishes are your score. The driver with the most points after 10 races is the champion.

Owner Championship — Your team's combined points across all its drivers. It's possible for one group to win both. It's also possible for your team to dominate while you personally have a rough season — depth matters.

Don't Have a Crew? Just Jump In

You don't need a group. Claim any available car and you're in. You'll automatically be part of whichever NASCAR team owns your number. If someone else grabs a car from the same team, you're teammates whether you planned it or not.

Season to Season

At the end of the season every car is released. You can re-sign your driver, switch teams, bring in new people, or shake up the whole roster. Team chemistry, rivalries, and bragging rights carry over. The league only gets better.

Missed Season 1?

Spots are first come, first served — up to 21 players. If Season 1 fills before you get in, you're first in line for Season 2. Life happens. Guys move in and out. There will always be a spot for the right people.

Section 1
Roster & Car Claims
One Car Per Person Core Rule

Every player claims exactly one car from the available pool. That car is yours for the entire season. You race as that driver — wherever they finish, that's your score.

First Come, First Served

Cars are available on a first come, first served basis. Once a car is claimed it is locked. No trades, no swaps mid-season.

Team Limits Max 3 Per Team

No more than 3 players can be on the same NASCAR team. Hendrick Motorsports and Joe Gibbs Racing each have 4 cars — one will always go unclaimed per team.

Mid-Season Joins Limited

Players can join mid-season by claiming any remaining open car. Mid-season joiners are eligible for the Driver Championship — they just start at a points disadvantage. They are not eligible for the Owner Championship and their points do not count toward team totals.

Injured Drivers

If a real-life driver gets injured, the game will place a substitute in that car. Your car number stays the same — you race whoever the game puts in it. No substitutions or car swaps allowed.

End of Season

After the championship race, all cars are released. Players can re-sign their driver or switch to any available car for the following season.

Section 4
Playoff Points
What Are Playoff Points?

Playoff points are earned throughout the regular season by winning races and stages. They don't affect your regular season standing — they only matter when the Chase begins. At the Chase reset, your playoff points are added directly on top of your 2000 base.

How to Earn Them

Race Win — +5 playoff points
Stage Win — +1 playoff point per stage won

These stack. Win a race and two stages in one weekend = +7 playoff points.

Section 5
The Chase
Top 10 Advance Playoff

After Race 7 (the Regular Season Finale), the top 10 drivers in the standings advance to the Chase. Everyone else is eliminated from championship contention — their final standings are recorded.

Points Reset

All 10 Chase drivers reset to 2000 points at the start of Race 8. From there it's a 3-race sprint to the championship. Whoever has the most points after Race 10 wins.

Section 6
Owner Championship
Team vs Team

The Owner Championship is a competition between the 7 NASCAR teams. Each team's score is the combined points total of all its active drivers in the league throughout the full season.

Eligibility

Only full-season players count toward the Owner Championship. Mid-season joiners do not contribute to team totals. They are still eligible for the Driver Championship individually.

How It's Decided

The team with the highest combined points after Race 10 wins the Owner Championship. The Chase reset does not affect Owner Championship scoring — team totals run on raw cumulative points all season long.

Section 7
Tiebreakers
If Two Drivers Finish Equal on Points

Ties are broken in this order — first criteria to separate them wins:

  • 2Most Top 5 Finishes
  • 3Most Top 10 Finishes
  • 4Best Single-Race Finish

Section 8
Misc Rules
Entry Free

There is no entry fee. Owning NASCAR 26 is the buy-in. Season 1 is bragging rights only.

Results & Scoring

Race results are pulled directly from the game. Points are entered by the commissioner within 24 hours of each race. Stage results are recorded at each stage break.

Commissioner's Word is Final

Any situation not covered by these rules is decided by the commissioner. Decisions are final.
